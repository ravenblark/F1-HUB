About This Project

Hi, I'm Raena. I'm a developer and student with a deep passion for Formula 1. I wanted to combine my technical skills with my love for the sport to create something special for the F1 community.

F1HUB was built out of pure passion for Formula 1. As a dedicated fan of the sport, I wanted to create a place where everything about the 2026 season lives in one beautiful, easy-to-use dashboard — from team lineups and driver stats to circuit breakdowns and race results.

Every detail on this site is carefully researched and designed to give fans the best experience possible. Whether you're a hardcore F1 nerd or just getting into the sport, F1HUB has something for you.

Thank you for being here. Buckle up, it's going to be an exciting season!
